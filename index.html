Internet nahi? Padhai phir bhi.

Ilm Card puts a full school term on one memory card. It plays on a basic Android phone, with no internet.

Multan, our studio

It starts in one small studio.

Teachers record every lecture of the term, following the Punjab board syllabus. Each lecture comes with a quiz and a homework task. Then the whole term is copied onto memory cards.

The road to Muzaffargarh

Then the cards take the road.

Our van leaves Multan and crosses the Chenab. Past the river the signal gets weaker, and mobile data costs more than most families can spare.

25.37 million children aged 5 to 16 are out of school in Pakistan.Source: Federal Education Minister, statement to the National Assembly.

The first village

First stop, the mobile shop.

Families pick up the card where they already buy phone balance. A scratch voucher and one SMS unlock the month's lessons. No bank account and no data needed.

Rs 500 a month. That is about Rs 17 a day.

The second village

The signal drops. The lessons keep playing.

Lectures run straight from the card. Once a month the van comes back to collect homework and bring new lessons. Where there is no signal at all, the term pass comes with its code sealed in the pack.

After dark

One phone becomes a classroom.

When the day's work is done, the children sit down together. They watch the lecture, take the quiz and finish the week's task. Their scores come back by SMS.

The next morning

Ilm bhi, tarbiyat bhi.

Every week, one short lesson is about character: honesty, respect, good manners, and looking after your home and your village. We want good students who are also good people.

How it works

  1. Get the card. One memory card holds a full term of lectures, quizzes and homework, following the Punjab board syllabus.
  2. Unlock it. Buy a scratch voucher at a mobile shop, send its number by SMS and get the month's code back.
  3. Learn offline. Lectures play inside the app on a basic Android phone. No internet is used.
  4. Hand in homework. Work goes back through the shop, an NGO centre or the monthly van. Scores come back by SMS.
  5. Sit the exam. Each term ends with an exam at a nearby school and a completion certificate.

  2. Months 3 to 6

  3. Months 6 to 12

Ilm Card is at the idea stage today. These are targets, and the pilot is how we will test them.
